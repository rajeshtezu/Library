# Evaluating Models for Code Gen and Business Tasks

## The Chinchilla Scaling Law

Number of parameters ~ proportional to the number of training tokens

If you are getting diminishing returns from training with more training data, then this law gives you a rule of thumb for scaling your model.

And vice versa: if you upgrade to a model with double the number of weights, this law suggests you need twice the data.

## How to evaluate the performance of a Gen AI solution

**Model-centric or Technical Metrics** (Easiest to optimize with) :
- Loss (e.g. cross-entropy loss)
- Perplexity
- Accuracy
- Precision, Recall, F1
- AUC-ROC

**Business-centric or Outcome Metrics** (Most tangible impact):
- KPIs tied ot business objectives
- ROI
- Improvements in time, cost or resources
- Customer satisfaction
- Benchmark comparisons
